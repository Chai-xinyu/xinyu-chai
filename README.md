Electrocatalysis and AI for Science. I work on turning literature into data,
and data into catalysts.

## Research

- **CO2 electroreduction to C2+ products** — Cu-based catalysts; facet control,
  surface functionalization, and the selectivity gap between CO and ethylene.
- **Biomass electro-upgrading** — HMF and furfural oxidation, and their coupling
  with CO2 reduction in membrane electrode assembly systems.
- **Oxide interfaces** — Lewis-acidic oxide design for local alkalinity,
  water dissociation, and oxygen-vacancy engineering.
- **AI4S infrastructure** — LLM-based extraction pipelines that convert papers
  into structured, queryable, reproducible datasets.

## Current focus

Building a literature-to-database workflow for anode oxidation and CO2 reduction:
schema-first extraction, DOI verification, and automated consistency checks.
The goal is simple — every number traceable to the paper it came from.
